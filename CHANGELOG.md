# Changelog

All notable changes to the Abnormal UI registry are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the version is the registry's, in `package.json`, following [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Each entry names the items it touches, because a project installs items one by one and takes a change only when it reinstalls that item. The README's "Versions and updates" section says how.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `icon-button`: `IconButton`, a Button that shows only an icon, whose required `label` is both its aria-label and the tooltip it shows on hover and keyboard focus. `tooltip` shows other words (the name with its shortcut), `tooltip={false}` none, `tooltipSide` the side it prefers; inside a TooltipTrigger it leaves the hint to the trigger. A client component; server items keep a Button with an aria-label.
- `tooltip`: `LabelTooltip`, a tooltip around one element in one line, for anything that used the browser's `title`; it describes the element unless its label repeats the element's aria-label, and around a disabled button it listens on a box around it, so a hint that says why the button is disabled still shows. `TooltipContent` now keeps 8px from the window's edges (`collisionPadding`) and hides while its trigger is scrolled out of a scrolling box (`hideWhenDetached`), on top of the portal and the flip to the other side it already had. A trigger opens its tooltip on keyboard focus only, so focus handed back by a menu or dialog after a click no longer pops it up.
- `eslint-plugin`: `abnormal/no-native-title` reports `title` on HTML elements and on Button, IconButton, Link, Toggle, ToggleGroupItem, ThemePicker and LanguagePicker; `abnormal/icon-button-label` reports a Button of an icon size outside a TooltipTrigger (use IconButton) and a bare `<button>` that shows no text outside a TooltipTrigger or LabelTooltip, unless the source hides it for certain (`hidden`, or `aria-hidden` with a negative `tabIndex`). The registry runs both on its components and site.
- `table`: `SortableHead`'s `title` (what the click does) shows in the theme's tooltip instead of the browser's title.
- `toolbar`: an icon-sized `ToolbarButton` shows its aria-label in the theme's tooltip.
- `theme-picker`, `language-picker`: the tooltip goes through `LabelTooltip`, so the default one, which repeats the button's name, is no longer also read as its description.

- `time-picker`: a time of day as the browser's own time input, typed segment by segment, with a clock button that opens a list of times in the theme's popover instead of the browser's picker, which Chrome and Edge draw in system blue whatever the theme. The list holds a time every quarter of an hour (every `step` when longer, or `listStep`), on the clock unless `min` falls between two steps, written the `locale`'s way, dims the times outside `min` and `max`, opens at the chosen time and takes the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up / Down, Enter, Escape and Tab; Alt+Arrow Down opens it from the input. The focused segment takes the highlighter instead of system blue. Valued as `"09:30"` like `<input type="time">`, it submits with a `name` and follows a form reset. `listLabel` names the list and its button.
- `app-frame`: a product window as one canvas, after Front's layout. `AppFrame` (the canvas, `bg-canvas`; a Sidebar on it drops its own fill), `AppFrameBody` (the row of columns, stacked on a small screen), `AppFrameColumn` (a list, a tool rail or facets straight on the canvas), `AppFrameSheet` (the one inset sheet: page color, panel radius, `shadow-sheet`, clipped without becoming a scroll container), `AppFrameSheetHeader` and `AppFrameSheetTitle` (the 44px first row), `AppFrameSheetBar` (the quieter second row) and `AppFrameDock` (a raised box at the sheet's foot for a reply or a note). Server-safe; `workspace` stays for the ink frame.
- `card-list`: small cards on the canvas, one per conversation, ticket or task. `CardList`, `CardListItem`, `CardListCard` (a link or button with `asChild`; `selected` lifts it onto the page color with a highlighter bar at its start, `unread` bolds the first line and lights `CardListDot`, whose `label` reads "Unread" to a screen reader), `CardListHeader`, `CardListTitle`, `CardListMeta`, `CardListLine` and `CardListPreview`. Server-safe.
- `sidebar`: `SidebarSwitcher`, the module at the top of the column as a menu trigger (its icon alone on the rail); `SidebarGroup`, a named set of rows with a small label (a hairline on the rail); and `SidebarMore`, which folds the rows used less under a More / Less row with `aria-expanded`.
- `list-toolbar`: `ListToolbarFilter`, a condition as a raised 32px chip (the field, the value in force and a chevron) that triggers a menu or popover, and with `add` the dashed chip that adds one.
- `eslint-plugin`: `eslint-plugin-abnormal.mjs`, installed to `eslint/`, with two rules that keep hand-picked values out of a product. `abnormal/no-hardcoded-classes` reads class strings and JSX style objects in TS and TSX: Tailwind's palette and arbitrary colors, `dark:` color overrides, arbitrary font sizes and whole-pixel spacing, Tailwind's own radii and shadows, numeric layers and letter spacing that has a name, each reported with the theme's name to use instead. `abnormal/no-hardcoded-css` does the same for declarations in CSS through `@eslint/css` (literal colors, px or rem font sizes, spacing of 3px or more, radii above 2px, z-index of 10 or more), leaving custom property definitions alone; it parses with `tailwind-csstree`'s Tailwind syntax , so `@theme`, `@utility` and `@apply` with variants are read in full and anything it cannot read yet fails as a parse error instead of passing unchecked. A value that has to stay takes a plain `abnormal-allow:` comment saying why, which lints the same where the plugin is not installed; the registry's components use it. Existing values can be recorded with `eslint --suppress-rule`, so only new ones fail. The registry runs both on its own components and site.
- `not-found`: the page a product shows for an address it does not have, the same on every Abnormal site. `NotFound` (the page's `main`: one narrow column centred in the window, opening with the brand mark unless `mark={false}`), `NotFoundCode` (the status code in the mono label), `NotFoundTitle` (the h1, or the page's heading with `asChild`), `NotFoundDescription`, `NotFoundActions`, and `NotFoundAside`, under a rule, for where else to go or the same words in the site's other language. Server-safe.
- `clip-track`: a strip of clips in playing order, each as wide as it is long, for a video's shots or the segments of a recording. `ClipTrack` (a named group), `ClipTrackRuler` (time marks from `total`, `ticks` and `format`), `ClipTrackLane` (an ordered list), `ClipTrackItem` (`length`, taking exactly its share of the width), `ClipTrackClip` (a button with `asChild`, `selected` in the muted highlighter with a signal border, `tone` and `progress` for the state bar along its foot) and `ClipTrackPlayhead` (`position` out of `total`), all on one linear time scale. `clipTrackMinWidth(lengths)` gives the track width at which the shortest clip is still 48px wide, for a page that scrolls the track sideways. Server-safe.
- `bento`: a wall of feature tiles for a landing page. `Bento` (one column, two from `sm`, three from `lg`; a `ul role="list"` with `asChild`), `BentoTile` (`wide` spans two columns from `sm`), `BentoTitle`, `BentoDescription` and `BentoVisual`, the picture pushed to the foot of the tile. Server-safe.
- `side-pane`: a pane beside a list that folds to a 40px rail and opens again, such as the facet column on the start side and the current row's details on the end side. `SidePane` (`side`, `collapsed` / `defaultCollapsed` / `onCollapsedChange`, and `cookieName` to keep the choice across reloads, as Sidebar does), `SidePaneHeader`, `SidePaneTitle` (running down the rail when folded), `SidePaneToggle` (aria-expanded and aria-controls, labelled by `collapseLabel` and `expandLabel`) and `SidePaneContent`, hidden but kept while folded.
- `list-toolbar`: the row over a list, the same on every list: `ListToolbar` (a `div`, or the page's `form` with `asChild`), `ListToolbarSearch`, `ListToolbarGroup`, `ListToolbarActions` at the far end and `ListToolbarChips`, the line of conditions in force. Server-safe.
- `table`: `density="compact"` for a dense list between side panes (shorter rows, closer cells, the smaller text), and `TableRow current` for the row a details pane shows, with `aria-current`, the hover color and a bar at its start edge.
- `status-bar`: the strip along the bottom of an app window. `StatusBar` is a `footer` (or the page's element with `asChild`) in the small secondary text with even-width numbers; `StatusBarItem`, a word or a count, or with `asChild` a link or button the bar's full height; `StatusBarSpacer`, `StatusBarSeparator`, and `StatusBarShortcut`, the key caps (`keys`) and what they do. It takes no position and no theme: the page places it and adds `ink` to continue an ink navigation column. Server-safe.
- `facet-list`: the column beside a list that narrows it, such as saved views, statuses with their counts or the domains the rows belong to. `FacetGroup` and `FacetGroupLabel`, `FacetList` and `FacetItem`, `FacetRow` (a link or button with `asChild`, `selected` in the muted highlighter, the page supplying `aria-current` or `aria-pressed`), `FacetLabel`, `FacetCount`, and `FacetMore`, the row that loads the next page of a long group. Server-safe.
- `list-navigation`: `useListNavigation` (`use-list-navigation.ts`), single-key navigation for a list with one current row: J and K move it, Enter or O opens it, Escape lets go and "/" focuses the list's search, with `onBoundary` past either end for paging. The keys are left alone in fields, dialogs, menus, with a modifier held and during an IME composition, and Enter stays a focused link's or button's.
- `workspace`: server-safe ink / paper composition primitives. `WorkspaceNavigation` carries the permanent ink scope; sibling `WorkspacePane` surfaces follow the page theme, with local `none`, `compact` or `comfortable` padding. Product navigation, widths, height and scrolling remain the consumer's responsibility.
- `/design`: the family style contract and live reading-workspace, operational-list and service-health compositions, with a discoverable Markdown alternate. The existing palette is preserved; product repositories are not changed by adopting this guide.
- `template`: `fillTemplate` and the `Template<Slot>` type, the copy strings with `{name}` slots that the website and the developer site each kept a copy of. The slots exist only in the type, so a component that passes a slot its copy does not declare, or leaves one out, fails the type check.
- `theme-script`: `ThemeScript`, the inline `<head>` script that applies the saved or system theme before the first paint, with `<meta name="theme-color">` in each theme's `--bg`, and follows the system and other tabs; and `useThemePreference` (`use-theme-preference.ts`), its preference and setter for `ThemePicker`. It keeps the choice under the same `abnormal-theme` key and `window.abnormalTheme` as the website and the developer site, which each kept a copy of the script.

### Changed

- `dialog`, `sheet`, `side-pane`, `selection-bar`, `calendar`: their icon buttons are IconButtons, so the close, fold, clear and month buttons show their names as tooltips; `search-input`'s clear button, `password-input`'s eye, `time-picker`'s clock and `copy-button`'s icon-only button show theirs through LabelTooltip, and none sets `title` any more.
- `theme-picker`, `language-picker`: an icon-only button now shows its `label` in the tooltip by default; `tooltip` gives other words and `false` none. `TooltipContent` portals into the full-screen element, or the topmost native modal `<dialog>`, while one is open, so a full-screen video player's buttons and a modal dialog's still show theirs above the browser's top layer.
- `uptime-bar`: a bar's `title` shows above it in the theme's tooltip, drawn with CSS so the strip still ships no script, leaning away from the strip's ends; `UptimeValue` takes `hint` for the same instead of `title`.
- `banner`, `tag`: `BannerClose` and the tag's × drop their `title`; they are server items, so they keep only the aria-label.
- `date-time-picker`: the time half is a `time-picker`, so its list of times opens in the theme's popover instead of the browser's picker. `timeListLabel` names that list and its button (`timeLabel` unless given), and `timeClassName` now goes to the time field's box.
- `date-picker`: an empty value (`""`, what an empty `<input type="date">` submits and what a page passes back from one) is no day, where it made the calendar throw when it opened. So is a day that does not exist, such as `"2026-13-10"` from a hand-edited link: the calendar opens on today, and the field submits no day, as a date input does.
- Every component follows the same feel: hover and press transitions take 0.1s instead of 0.15s in `accordion`, `breadcrumb`, `calendar`, `card`, `checkbox`, `combobox`, `date-picker`, `date-range-picker`, `disclosure`, `expandable-text`, `info-hint`, `input`, `input-group`, `input-otp`, `native-select`, `nav-tabs`, `page-header`, `prose`, `radio-group`, `resize-handle`, `scroll-area`, `search-input`, `skip-link`, `slider`, `switch`, `table` (row actions included), `tabs`, `text-link`, `textarea`, `toc` and `toggle`. `input`, `textarea`, `native-select`, `input-group`, `input-otp`, `checkbox` and `radio-group` sit on the popover color (white in light) instead of the control fill. The `combobox`, `date-picker` and `date-range-picker` triggers are raised like the `select` trigger. `kbd` `raised` uses the popover color, the `switch` thumb takes `shadow-control`, the `slider` thumb no longer grows when pressed, `alert-dialog` drops the backdrop blur and uses the dialog's 20px padding, and `sheet` uses 20px sides.
- `theme`: `--canvas` and `bg-canvas` (the surface in light, the sidebar color in dark) behind an `app-frame`, and `--sheet-shadow` / `shadow-sheet`, the soft edge of its sheet, registered in `cn` (`utils`) and named by `eslint-plugin`. No color value changes.
- `nav-tabs`: `segmented` is a sunken track whose current tab is raised with `shadow-control`, matching `toggle-group`.
- `dialog`: the footer's bleed matches the 20px padding, so it no longer reaches past the panel's edges. `select`: a group adds no padding of its own inside the popover's. `theme`: toasts take the 8px `--radius-mark`. `tabs` `chip` and the small `toggle-group` outline segment keep a 24px floor in compact density.
- `theme`: `--control-shadow` and `shadow-control`, one soft pixel under a raised control, registered in `cn` (`utils`) and named by `eslint-plugin` next to `shadow-panel` and `shadow-popover`. Floating layers arrive in 0.12s and travel 2px instead of 0.15s and 4px, and leave in 0.1s.
- `button`: `outline` is a raised surface (the popover color, the input border, `shadow-control`, full-strength text) whose shadow flattens while pressed. No variant scales when pressed any more; `default`, `highlight` and `secondary` deepen their fill instead.
- `dropdown-menu`, `context-menu`, `select`, `command`: 28px rows of 13px text with a 4px radius inside a popover with 4px of padding; group labels in the 11px text; shortcuts in the 11px mono face. The `select` trigger is raised like an `outline` button.
- `tooltip`: a small ink label in both themes, using the `.ink` values.
- `toggle-group`: `variant="outline"` is a segmented control, a sunken track at the control height with the pressed segment raised on it in the popover color.
- `tabs`: `variant="chip"`, small 24px outlined chips with the current one filled gray, for the quick filters above a list.
- `dialog`: no backdrop blur, 20px of padding and a 16px gap. `sonner`: toasts take the menu radius.
- Reinstall `theme` and `utils` together with any item below: the components now use utilities (`bg-overlay`, `z-overlay`, `text-warning`, `tracking-heading` and the rest) that only this theme defines, and that only this `utils` merges, so a component reinstalled over an older theme loses its backdrop and its layer.
- `utils`: `cn` knows the letter spacing names and the stacking layers, so `cn("z-overlay", "z-40")` keeps the caller's `z-40` and `cn("tracking-heading", "tracking-tight")` the caller's spacing.
- `timeline`, `prose`: the dot's offset and the heading margin are spacing steps (`1.75`, `13`), the same sizes in the default density.
- `theme`: semantic names for the values products were writing by hand. `warning` (the amber of something pending or about to happen; `badge-ink` stays as an alias), `overlay` (the dim behind a dialog or sheet: black at 40%, 70% in dark mode), `border-strong` (a hovered card, an empty-state outline) and `input-hover` (an input's border on hover and focus), with `--success-soft` / `-line`, `--warning-soft` / `-line` and `--destructive-soft` / `-line` for handwritten CSS. Eight named category hues, `category-gray` to `category-purple`, each with a dot color, a `-soft` fill and an `-ink` text color (the mail label palette), for labels, calendars and file types. Letter spacing `tracking-title` (-0.01em), `tracking-heading` (-0.02em, also the h1-h4 default), `tracking-display` (-0.03em) and `tracking-caps` (0.06em). A stacking scale, `z-raised` (1), `z-sticky` (10), `z-header` (20), `z-chrome` (30, an app shell's pinned parts and drag feedback above a sticky header), `z-overlay` (50), `z-floating` (60) and `z-skip-link` (70).
- `theme`: figures are proportional by default. The body no longer sets `tabular-nums`, which in Inter also widened hyphens and dashes, so dates and words such as "sign-in" read as monospaced. Components that show columns of numbers (stat, status-bar, pagination, badge counts, facet-list, item-list, sidebar counts, timeline, banner, uptime-bar, clip-track, kbd) already set `tabular-nums` themselves; a product's own numeric columns should too.
- `dialog`, `alert-dialog`, `sheet`: the backdrop is `bg-overlay`, so the sheet's is now as dark as a dialog's (it was 30% / 60%).
- `input`, `textarea`, `native-select`, `input-group`, `input-otp`, `card`: hover and focus borders use `input-hover` and `border-strong` instead of inline color mixes; `input-group`'s hover border is now the same as an input's.
- `banner`, `progress`, `status-label`, `timeline`, `uptime-bar`, `field-message`, `clip-track`: the amber tone is `warning`; `dialog`, `alert-dialog`, `sheet`, `popover`, `dropdown-menu`, `context-menu`, `select`, `tooltip`, `page-header`, `resize-handle`, `toggle-group`, `copy-button`, `skip-link`: z-index from the stacking scale (same values as before); `section-heading`, `bento`, `pricing-card`, `brand-mark`: letter spacing from the tracking names.
- `theme`: a compact density. `data-ui-density="compact"` on `<html>`, or on one region, with a fine pointer (touch screens keep the default), takes the spacing step from 4px to 3.5px (every Tailwind length built on it, component heights included: a 32px button or input is 28px), steps the interface type down about 1px (body 13px, `text-sm` 12.5px, `text-3xl` 24px; `text-2xs` and the display scale unchanged) and closes line heights to 1.45. The type scale and `--leading-body` moved from `@theme inline` to `@theme`, so the text utilities read them as variables (`font-size: var(--text-sm)`) and a density can change them; the body and toasts take `--text-base` and `--leading-body`. `button` (`xs`, `icon-xs`), `search-input`'s clear button and `info-hint` keep a 24px floor, the WCAG target minimum, under the compact density. Without the attribute nothing changes except the `sm` search box: its clear button is 24px at either density (20px before), and its end padding grows from 28px to 32px so the button never covers typed text.
- `section-heading`: `PageTitle`, the h1 at the top of an inner page (36px on phones to 56px on wide screens), and `PageLead`, the sentence under it, so a site's inner pages share one title scale.
- `button`: `variant="highlight"`, the highlighter fill with ink text in both themes, for the one action a marketing page leads with.
- `theme-script`: `ThemeScript` takes `defaultTheme` (`"system"`, the default, `"light"` or `"dark"`) for a site whose visitors start on one theme until they choose; `createThemeScript(defaultTheme)` returns that script. A choice equal to the default is not stored, and `<meta name="theme-color">` starts in the default's color.
- `item-list`: opt-in `density="compact"` row spacing and a `selected` muted-highlight treatment. Default spacing is unchanged. The product supplies selection semantics (`aria-current`, `aria-pressed`, or its widget's role); the visual prop does not invent them. Row transitions now respect reduced motion.

### Fixed

- `tooltip`: a `LabelTooltip` (and so an `IconButton`) no longer takes over its child's `data-state` and `data-slot`. A pressed `Toggle` or `ToggleGroupItem` kept its pressed look only until a tooltip went round it, a menu trigger lost `data-state="open"`, and a button styled by its `data-slot` lost that style; the trigger's own attributes now stop between the tooltip and the child. A click on an open tooltip no longer reaches what its trigger sits in (React passes portal events up the component tree), so a hint inside a clickable row does not open the row. A label that starts or stops repeating the child's name, and a disabled child's tooltip as it opens, no longer mount the child again. A child that a server component passes in, which arrives as a lazy reference with no props yet, no longer fails the server render ("Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'aria-label')").
- `toolbar`: `ToolbarButton` takes `tooltip`, for a hint that says more than the name, such as its shortcut, in place of a native `title`.
- `eslint-plugin`: `abnormal/icon-button-label` reads the content of a button's braces: an icon picked by a condition (`{ended ? <RotateIcon /> : <PlayIcon />}`) or shown by `&&` is not text, so such a button is reported; `{" "}` is not text either. A button with `hidden="false"` counts as hidden, as the browser hides it. `abnormal/no-native-title` also covers CopyButton, ToolbarButton, Badge, StatusLabel, TableHead and TableCell, which pass `title` to the element they render.
- `table`, `scroll-area`: a click in a list that scrolls no longer leaves a ring around it once a key is pressed. A click on a row focuses the scrolling container (a tab stop, so the arrow keys can scroll it), and Chrome matches `:focus-visible` from the next key, whatever it is, so Escape or J after a click drew the container's ring, half hidden under the sticky header and the pinned column. `usePointerFocus` (in `use-scroll-fade.ts`) marks focus that came from a click with `data-pointer-focus`, on the container or on an element inside it, and both components hide their ring while it is there; a page's own rows can do the same with `:focus-visible:not([data-pointer-focus])`. The table's keyboard ring is now drawn over the part of the table in view, above the sticky header and the pinned columns, so it is whole. `table` now depends on `scroll-area` for the hook.
- `tabs`: a click on a tab no longer scrolls the page. Radix focuses the tab on mousedown, and in a sticky tab row the browser scrolled the page back to the row's resting place, so on a long page scrolled down the row jumped on every click. `TabsTrigger` now focuses it with `preventScroll` first. The arrow keys, Home and End did the same through Radix's roving focus, so a horizontal `TabsList` now moves between its tabs itself, with Radix's keys and rules (disabled tabs skipped, wrapping unless `loop={false}`, arrows swapped right to left), focusing without scrolling the page. Both scroll the row sideways just far enough to show the tab, and scroll the page only to bring back a tab that is outside the window, as a row in a stuck position never is.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-14

The pieces the projects kept building for themselves: page structure, lists and their states, navigation, and the fields that were still native inputs. Twenty-eight new items bring the registry to 93, and the items that render without a client module no longer bring one in through their icons. One package was added: `uqr`, for `qr-code`.

### Added

- `empty-state`: what a list, a search or a page shows when it has nothing to show: an icon in a tile, a title, a sentence and the next steps. `default` is the large centered block that stands in for a whole list; `compact` the dashed box for one panel among others. The title is a paragraph unless `asChild` makes it the heading the outline needs.
- `page-header`: the top of a product page: the h1, an ⓘ or a line saying what the page is for, the page's actions at 32px, and `PageHeaderBack`, a link back to the parent page (or a `breadcrumb` in its place). `sticky` keeps it at the top on an opaque background, and draws its line while the page sets `data-stuck`. `PageHeaderActions` wraps its actions, or with `scroll` keeps them on one line that scrolls sideways for a phone; `asChild` puts the row on a `ScrollFade` so its cut-off edges fade.
- `description-list`: names and their values in a `dl`, the values starting on one line (`auto minmax(0,1fr)`), as a bare list, a tinted `panel`, or `spread` rows for a receipt. `DescriptionDetails` takes an `action` beside the value, such as a `CopyButton`. `lined` keeps the values in their column with a hairline between rows, each value going under its name on a phone; `stacked` puts every value under its name.
- `timeline`: events in order on a rail, each marked by a dot in its tone or, given `icon`, a round tile, with `TimelineTitle`, `TimelineTime` (a `<time>`) and `TimelineContent`. The list is named by the page.
- `banner`: a strip across the top of a page or pane about the state of the whole thing (offline, an auto-reply that is on, maintenance), or with `variant="panel"` a tinted block of its own, such as a status page's verdict. Five tones, each tinting the background; `BannerText`, `BannerDot` in the tone's color, `BannerMeta`, `BannerAction` and `BannerClose`, a dismiss button named by `label`. A status region unless given `role="alert"`. Icons come in as children, so the item imports no client module.
- `stat`: the headline numbers of a page: `StatGrid`, a `dl` of tiles or of `joined` cells, two to a row on a phone and up to `columns`; `Stat` with a label and a value; `StatHint`; and `StatDelta`, the change, with a direction arrow and a tone that says whether it is good news.
- `kbd`: `Kbd`, a key cap in two sizes, and `KbdCombo`, keys pressed together. `use-modifier-key.ts` brings `useModifierKey`, which tells a Mac's ⌘ from Control after hydration and leaves the printed name to the page.
- `selection-bar`: the toolbar above a list that acts on the rows checked in it: a select-all checkbox that shows none, some or all, the count in the page's words (also a status region), the actions while something is checked, and a clear button. While nothing is checked, `idle` can take the count's place, such as the list's filter tabs. Without `onSelectAll` the bar has no checkbox of its own, for a list whose select-all sits in its table header. While `disabled`, the actions are disabled with the checkbox and the clear button.
- `item-list`: a short bordered list with a line between rows; `ItemListRow` is plain, or a whole-row link or button with `asChild`, with a title, a description, `ItemListMeta` and `ItemListChevron`.
- `field`: `Field` wires a `FieldLabel`, the control in a `FieldControl` and a `FieldHint` together (`htmlFor`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`). `error` makes the control invalid and shows in the hint's place, announced at once. `orientation="row"` is the bordered setting row, and `SwitchField` that row with a `Switch`.
- `tag`: a chip for a chosen value with an × button named by `removeLabel`, and `TagList` to hold them.
- `truncated-text`: a line cut with an ellipsis, or a few with `line-clamp-*`, that shows the whole text in a tooltip only when it was cut.
- `expandable-text`: a long text clamped to `lines`, with a show more / show less button (`moreLabel`, `lessLabel`) only when it runs past them, measured again when its width changes.
- `uptime-bar`: `UptimeBar`, a strip of up, degraded, down and empty bars, each with its `title` as a native tooltip, named as one image and drawn with no script; `UptimeValue`, the uptime in its color; and `uptimeStatus`, a bucket's status from its counts.
- `code-block`: a `pre` that scrolls sideways and takes focus, with an optional header naming it and `action`, a corner slot for a `CopyButton`, which copies the code beside it, so the code reaches the page once. The block imports no button, so a page that shows code without one ships no script for it.
- `qr-code`: a QR code drawn as inline SVG on the server, dark on white in both themes, named as an image. It brings `uqr`, the encoder the storefront already uses: a QR encoder is not something to own here.
- `skip-link`: the first link on a page, above the top edge until it takes the keyboard focus. Its shadow shows only with the focus, so nothing smudges the corner while it waits.
- `sidebar`: an app's column that collapses to a 64px icon rail and expands to 248px rows: `SidebarHeader`, `SidebarNav` (a landmark named by `label`), `SidebarSeparator`, `SidebarFooter`, `SidebarToggle`, and `SidebarItem`, a row with an icon, a name, a `count` read as `countLabel`, `attention` and a `dot`, a button or, with `asChild`, the router's link. On the rail the name and count move to a tooltip and stay in the row's name. The state is the sidebar's or the page's (`collapsed`, `onCollapsedChange`); `cookieName` writes it to a cookie the server can read into `defaultCollapsed`, and `shortcut` toggles it with ⌘ or Ctrl, not while typing. `SidebarProvider` shares the state with things outside the column, and `use-sidebar.ts` brings `useSidebar` for a project's own rows.
- `nav-tabs`: tabs made of links, for views with their own address: `NavTabs`, a landmark named by `label`, and `NavTab`, a link or with `asChild` the router's, marked with `aria-current` (`"page"`, or `"location"` for a section of the page). Three looks: `underline`, `pill` and `segmented`. The row scrolls sideways without a scrollbar, fades its cut-off edges, takes a vertical wheel and brings the current tab into view when it changes.
- `toolbar`: `Toolbar`, `role="toolbar"` named by `label`, with one tab stop and the arrow keys, Home and End between its controls, past disabled and hidden ones; `ToolbarGroup`, `ToolbarSeparator`, `ToolbarButton` (ghost, icon-sized, a toggle with `pressed`) and `ToolbarItem` for a select or a menu trigger.
- `resize-handle`: `ResizeHandle`, a focusable `role="separator"` named by `label` that sets a pane's width (`orientation="vertical"`) or height (`"horizontal"`): a pointer drag, the arrow keys by `step`, Home and End to `min` and `max`, and a double-click for `onReset`. `onValueChange` follows each move and `onValueCommit` comes once, to save. `use-pointer-drag.ts` brings `usePointerDrag`, the drag with pointer capture, for a handle of a project's own.
- `toc`: a table of contents beside a document: `Toc`, a landmark named by `label` that marks the section being read with `aria-current="location"` as the page scrolls, measured against the page's `scroll-padding-top` and each heading's `scroll-margin-top`; `TocTitle`, `TocList` and `TocLink` with a `level`. The links render on the server and work without a script.
- `language-picker`: `LanguagePicker`, the languages button and menu, and `LanguageOptions` for a menu the page already has. Like `theme-picker` it holds no state: the page passes the current language and writes back the choice, or gives each option the `href` of its page (with `hreflang`). Each name carries its own `lang`; `showValue` writes the current one beside the icon; `tooltip`, `side` and `align` as on `theme-picker`.
- `date-time-picker`: a day and a time of day in one field, a `date-picker` and the browser's time input, valued as `"2026-11-10T09:30"` (with seconds when `step` is under a minute), the text `<input type="datetime-local">` submits. It is empty until both halves are set; `defaultTime` fills the time when a day comes first; `min` and `max` hold the calendar to their days and the time to their times; `name` submits it as one field, and a form reset puts it back. `clearable` with `clearLabel` empties the day and the time together. `formatValue` writes the day on its button the page's way, as on `date-picker`; the time is the browser's own input.
- `file-button`: a `Button` that opens a hidden file input (`accept`, `multiple`, `capture`) and hands the files to `onFiles`, emptying the input so the same file can be chosen again; with a `name` the input keeps them and submits with its form.
- `password-input`: an `InputGroup` password field with a show and hide button named by `showLabel` and `hideLabel`, an eye icon or with `toggle="text"` the words, `visible` for a page that holds the state, and `actions` for more buttons such as generate. A form reset hides the password again.
- `turnstile`: Cloudflare Turnstile for a form: `useTurnstile` (in `use-turnstile.ts`) loads the script once per page, renders the widget in the page's theme, writes the token into a hidden field (`fieldName`), and gives `status`, `waiting`, `token()`, `reset()` and `guard`, which stops a submission that comes before the token. `Turnstile` is the same as a component, with `resetKey`. A failed check leaves the decision to the server.
- `json-ld`: `JsonLd`, a page's structured data as a native `<script type="application/ld+json">`, and `serializeJsonLd`, the JSON with every `<` escaped so a value cannot end the element.

### Changed

- `badge`: a `size`, with `sm` the 20px chip for a dense row. `BadgeCount` is a number in a round chip, capped at `max` with a plus ("99+") and hidden at zero; `BadgeDot` a dot that says there is something new, decoration unless given a `label`. Badges without `size` are as before.
- `text-link`: `variant="inline"`, the link inside a sentence: the foreground color with a faint underline that deepens on hover. Now needs `class-variance-authority`.
- `prose`: styles for code blocks, tables, block quotes and rules, for documentation rendered from Markdown. A table scrolls sideways inside the column instead of widening the page. A `code-block` inside keeps its own frame: only a bare `<pre>` takes the panel, and the block takes the same spacing.
- `pricing-card`: `discount`, the offer as a chip under the price, and `originalPrice` with `originalPriceLabel`, the price before it, struck through and read out first by a screen reader. `PricingPrice` is the price block on its own (amount, period, offer, struck price), for a product page, an order panel or a table cell (`size="sm"`), with `highlight` for the Marker; the card renders it, unchanged. `icon` sits above the name.
- `progress`: `tone`, `warning` or `destructive`, for a usage meter near or at its limit, and `muted`, a grey bar for work that has stopped or finished.
- `sheet`: `SheetClose`, and `SheetPanel`, the bare portalled panel for a sheet that places and styles itself.
- `table`: `SortableHead`, a header cell whose link sorts the list on the server, with `aria-sort` and an arrow for the direction. Now needs `lucide-react` and `radix-ui`.
- `copy-button`: `text` is optional. Without it the button copies the code beside it when pressed: the text of the first `<code>` (else `<pre>`) in its parent, with each `<br>` read as a line break, so a code block rendered on the server sends its source once instead of again as a prop. With nothing beside it the copy fails as a refused clipboard does. Buttons that pass `text` work as before.
- `copy-button`: without `write`, a page with no Clipboard API (one served over plain HTTP, such as an internal address) or one whose clipboard refuses now copies the older way, by selecting the text in a hidden textarea next to the button and calling `execCommand("copy")`; the focus goes back to the button. It reports a failure only when that fails too. `onCopied` (with the text) and `onCopyFailed` (with the error) hear the outcome, for a page that also says it in its own words.
- `scroll-area`: `fade` fades the edges that have more content past them, and `strip` makes a one-line row that scrolls sideways, a vertical mouse wheel included. `use-scroll-fade.ts` brings the two hooks behind them, `useScrollFade` and `useWheelSideways`, for a row of a project's own, and `ScrollFade` is a plain element with both, for a row that keeps the browser's own scrolling, such as `PageHeaderActions` with `asChild`.
- `tabs`: `TabsList` takes `variant="pill"`, the tabs as pills with the current one in the highlighter and no hairline, for the sections of a settings panel or tabs inside a card.
- `sonner`: a `className`, `style`, `icons` or `toastOptions` given to `Toaster` now adds to the toaster's own instead of replacing it, and `toastOptions.classNames.toast` joins `cn-toast`, so a page no longer has to repeat that class.
- `theme-picker`: `tooltip` and `tooltipSide` add a tooltip to the button, and `side` places the menu, for a sidebar's footer. With `value` undefined the button now shows the sun or the moon for the theme the page is painted in, instead of the system icon, so no wrong icon shows before the preference is known. Now needs `tooltip`. It also carries `"use client"`: it renders `dropdown-menu`, a client module, and takes `onValueChange`, so it only ever ran in a client component, and the directive says so.
- `date-picker`: `clearable`, with the required `clearLabel`, for a date that may be left empty, such as an expiry: while a day is chosen, a button under the calendar empties the field, which then submits an empty value and calls `onValueChange` with `""`. A form reset still brings the default back. Now needs `button`. `formatValue` writes the chosen day on the button the page's way, from the value as the field holds it (`"2026-11-14"`), in place of Intl; the calendar keeps the locale's names.
- `date-range-picker`: `clearable` with `clearLabel` empties the range, and `onValueChange` then gets null. `allowPartial`, with the required `fromOnlyLabel` and `toOnlyLabel`, lets one end stand alone, such as the start of an import: after the first day the popover offers to keep it as the start only or the end only, the value is `{ from, to: null }` or `{ from: null, to }` (`OpenDateRange`), the button shows the day with a dash on the open side, and the missing end submits as an empty field. Without either, a range is both ends or nothing, as before. Now needs `button`. `formatValue` writes each end the page's way, from the day as the field holds it, with the dash between them and on an open side.

### Fixed

- `breadcrumb`, `disclosure`, `pagination`, `stepper`, `pricing-card`: no longer import `lucide-react`, whose 1.x `Icon` is a client module, so a Server Component page that shows them, and nothing interactive, loads no client chunk for an icon; `disclosure` now needs no client JavaScript at all, as its notes say. Their icons are drawn inline with lucide's own paths, size and stroke, the same SVG without lucide's `lucide` and `lucide-<name>` classes, and they no longer need `lucide-react`. The new items without a client module (`banner`, `page-header`, `stat`, `item-list`, `tag`, `code-block` and the rest) are built the same way.
- `calendar`: a key that moves nowhere, such as Home on the first day of the week or an arrow past `min` or `max`, no longer leaves the grid waiting to move the focus. The next render of the calendar, whatever caused it, used to pull the focus back into the grid from the field the person had moved on to.
- `command`: `CommandEmpty` shows on the first render of a list with no options. It looked for its options before the `Command` around it had attached its element, so an empty list showed nothing until the search changed.
- `command`: a `ref` given to `Command` now receives its root element alongside the one the component keeps. It used to take that one's place, so the highlight, the arrow keys and the reset to the first match after a search stopped working.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-11

A search box, so a project no longer shows the clear button each browser draws inside `type="search"`. One new item brings the registry to 65. No package was added.

### Added

- `search-input`: `Input` with the search icon at its start and a clear button at its end while it holds text. The button is the interface's close icon, the same in every browser, with a name (`clearLabel`, required) and a place in the tab order; the browser's own clear button is hidden. Clearing fires `onChange` with an empty value, controlled or not, then `onClear`, and puts the focus back in the box. `hint` shows in the same corner while the box is empty, such as the shortcut that focuses it. It takes `Input`'s three sizes and follows a form reset.

### Upgrading from 0.3.1

Nothing has to change. To adopt the item, install `search-input` and replace an `Input type="search"` and the icon beside it with `SearchInput`: `className` now sizes the box around the input, and `inputClassName` reaches the input.

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-11

Fixes to the form reset and to the calendar's edges that 0.3.0 left open. No item and no package was added.

### Fixed

- `combobox`, `date-picker`: a form reset now reaches a field that has no `name`, or is `disabled`. The field found its form through the hidden input it submits with, which such a field does not render; the button finds it now, as in `date-range-picker`, so the button also takes the `form` attribute. A controlled field still follows its `value` prop, and a cancelled reset still leaves the field alone.
- `input-otp`: a form reset puts an uncontrolled field back on its `defaultValue`, without calling `onValueChange`, unless the page cancels the reset. React keeps the input's value attribute on the typed code, so the browser's own reset changed nothing.
- `calendar`: the grid's tab stop, which is where a `date-picker` or `date-range-picker` puts the focus when it opens, is now a day within `min` and `max`. Today, or a chosen day, outside them used to take it.
- `calendar`: without `min` or `max`, the month buttons and the keyboard stop at January of year 1 and December 9999, the limits of the year list. The previous button went on into year 0, and December 9999, or a step past it from the keyboard, made the calendar throw. The cells after 31 December 9999 are empty.
- `calendar`: `addDays` and `addMonths` in `calendar-date.ts` stop at `0001-01-01` and `9999-12-31` (exported as `FIRST_DATE` and `LAST_DATE`) instead of returning text that is not a day.

### Changed

- `input-otp`: now needs `form-reset`.

### Upgrading from 0.3.0

Reinstalling `input-otp` brings `form-reset` with it; name it in the same command when reinstalling with `--overwrite`. Reinstall `calendar` to fix `date-picker` and `date-range-picker` too: the grid is its file. Nothing else has to change.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-10

Date ranges for the admin console's filters, a caption that jumps to a month and a year, and form resets that respect a cancelled reset. Two new items bring the registry to 64. No package was added: the range, the lists and the hook build on `Intl`, the browser's own `<select>` (through `native-select`) and React.

### Added

- `calendar`: `mode="range"` chooses a span of days as `{ from, to }`, both days as text. The first choice is one end, the days up to the pointer or the keyboard focus preview the range, and the second choice closes it, in order whichever end came first. The grid is `aria-multiselectable` and every day of the range is `aria-selected`; `min`, `max` and `isDateDisabled` apply to both ends. `calendar-date.ts` brings the `DateRange` and `ClosedDateRange` types and `orderRange`.
- `calendar`, `date-picker`: `monthLabel` and `yearLabel`, given together, turn the caption into two lists that jump to any month and year. The names are the caller's, the month names and digits the locale's, and the grid keeps its six weeks.
- `date-range-picker`: a field for a span of days, built from `popover` and `calendar`. It changes only once both ends are chosen, writes the range the way the locale does, and with `fromName` and `toName` submits the two days as a pair of hidden form fields.
- `form-reset`: `useFormReset`, the hook `switch`, `combobox`, `date-picker` and `date-range-picker` share to follow a form reset. A project's own field can use it.

### Changed

- `slider`: `thumbLabels` is required by the types when `value` or `defaultValue` is known to hold two numbers or more (a literal such as `defaultValue={[10, 40]}`, or a tuple type), with one name per thumb. A single thumb, and a `number[]` held in state, whose length the types cannot see, are as before. A range written as a literal without `thumbLabels` now fails the type check: give it its names.
- `calendar`: now needs `native-select`, for the two lists. The single-day props and markup are unchanged.
- `switch`, `combobox`, `date-picker`: now need `form-reset`.

### Fixed

- `switch`, `combobox`, `date-picker`: a form reset the page cancels (`<form onReset={(event) => event.preventDefault()}>`) no longer puts the field back on its default while the native fields beside it keep their values. The field now waits until the reset event has finished and acts only if nobody cancelled it.

### Upgrading from 0.2.0

Reinstalling `switch`, `combobox`, `date-picker` or `calendar` brings their new items with them (`form-reset`, `native-select`); name those in the same command when reinstalling with `--overwrite`. Nothing else has to change, unless a range `Slider` was written with a literal value and no `thumbLabels`, which the type check now reports.
